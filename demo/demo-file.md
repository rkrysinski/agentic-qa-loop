# Meridian Coffee Roasters — Q3 2024 Operational Summary

## Financial Performance
Revenue for Q3 2024 reached $2.4M, a 18% increase year-over-year.
Gross margin improved to 62%, up from 57% in Q3 2023.
Operating expenses totalled $980K, driven primarily by a new roasting facility lease signed in July.

## Production
Total beans roasted: 48,000 kg across four single-origin varieties:
- Ethiopia Yirgacheffe (12,000 kg)
- Colombia Huila (14,000 kg)
- Guatemala Antigua (10,000 kg)
- Kenya AA (12,000 kg)

The new roasting facility in Portland increased daily throughput capacity by 35%.

## Retail & Wholesale
Retail subscriptions grew to 8,200 active customers, up 22% from Q2.
Wholesale accounts expanded from 140 to 178 café partners across the Pacific Northwest.
The top-performing SKU was the Ethiopia Yirgacheffe 250g bag, accounting for 31% of retail revenue.

## Headcount
Total employees: 43 (up from 36 in Q2).
Two senior roasters were hired in August to support increased production volume.

## Outlook
Q4 target: $2.8M revenue. Key initiatives include launching a holiday gift bundle and onboarding 20 additional wholesale accounts.
